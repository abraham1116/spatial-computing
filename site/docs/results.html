Results

Figures measured on 14 November 2026 on a 2017 MacBook Pro (Intel i7-7660U, 2 cores, 16 GB, macOS 13.7.8), on AC power.

ConditionsA single machine, 1 to 5 repetitions, no confidence interval. On a low battery the same tests were about three times slower: the processor is throttled.
TestResult

Integrated GPU (Metal, float32)20 to 33× the two cores, against a non-vectorized CPU reference

Tiers + SSD33.5 MB of local RAM for 134 MB; 80.5 µs/access against 0.76 µs
